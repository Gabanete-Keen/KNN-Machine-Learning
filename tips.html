Wellness tips

Small, kind practices. Pick one that feels doable — that's enough.

Breathe

Try box breathing: in for 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. Repeat four times.

Rest

Keep a consistent bedtime and dim screens an hour before sleep.

Move

A 10-minute walk can lift mood and ease tension.

Connect

Send a message to someone you care about today.

Nourish

Drink water regularly and eat steady, simple meals.

Unplug

Set small windows of time away from news and notifications.

Reflect

Write down one thing that went okay today, however small.

Ask for help

Talking with a counsellor or doctor is a sign of strength.

If you feel unsafe or in crisis, please contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline right away.
